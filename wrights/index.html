The class campaigns didn't stop working on Oct 1. What Google counts changed.

Through Sep 30, Google counted every parent who clicked through to iClassPro as a conversion. From Oct 1, Gymnastics and NinjaZone count only completed purchases, and the Birthday Party campaign counts the Book Party button. Parents still reach iClassPro every day, but fewer than in late September (see chart).

Status

What's working, what to watch, and what needs a decision.

Working

Traffic is cheap and steady

About $1.54 a click across search over the last 30 days, and parents reach iClassPro from every campaign every day.

Watch

Fewer parents reaching sign-up

Class campaigns sent about 9 parents a day to iClassPro in late September and about 6 a day in October, on similar spend.

Needs a decision

Too few purchases to bid on

Class campaigns now bid only on purchases: about 2 in 13 days, most with no dollar value. Google can't learn from that little data.

Next steps

  1. Give the class campaigns enough signal. Keep purchases as the goal but add the iClassPro click back as a primary goal with a small value, until purchases reach about 30 a month.
  2. Send real purchase values. Most tracked purchases arrive at $0, so "maximize conversion value" has nothing to maximize.
  3. Report party results as button clicks. Book Party clicks are intent, not bookings. iClassPro doesn't report party bookings to Google.
